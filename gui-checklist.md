# GUI checklist for the movie maker

Fifteen blocks, one box each. Tick a block when everything listed in it works; when something does not, write what you saw under that block. The bullets say what to do and what to expect.

Everything was written without being able to run the GUI. Start from the example workspace `docs/examples/aspirin_phospholipase_movie.via` (put it next to `aspirin-phospholipase.gro`, `.xtc` and `.edr`, then **File > Open Workspace**). A second example, `docs/examples/aspirin_binding_movie.via`, uses the same files and shows a dolly zoom (16 to 24 s), bullet time (25 to 30.5 s) and representation hand-overs; it is worth a look after blocks 6 and 11 to 13. Open **Windows > Movie**: the timeline is on the left and the tabbed controls on the right. The manual is [`docs/movie_maker.md`](docs/movie_maker.md).

Already checked by you (blocks 1 to 5) are ticked.

## A. Editor, workspace, overlays (checked)

- [x] **1. Editor layout and workspace.** Divider, independent scrolling, hide **Timeline** / **Controls** (one always stays), shared playback and **Add Keyframe**; six tabs with their own controls; controls disabled while recording except Stop/Pause/Resume. Open the example (no log errors, 80 s, playhead at about 55.45 s); save under another name and reopen: keys, overlays, markers, representation keys, output settings and lanes are all back. Older workspaces (no representation ids, no overlays, keys from before the follow target, old one-figure plot overlays) load and convert.
- [x] **2. Overlays: text, time stamp, scale bar, logo, image, time bar.** A new movie has the logo (removing it sticks after save/open); transparent, sharp, readable on light and dark. Position, size (% or points, no jump when switching, same size in points at 1080p and 4K), color, background plate, fades, **Duplicate**, change **Type**, undo/redo all work. The scale bar follows zoom. The time stamp starts at 0, only grows (also backward) and shows time since the start when shown late. The image loads with transparency and jpg, path survives moving the workspace, a missing file turns red without a crash. The time bar fills forward whichever way the trajectory plays, with grey track and plate. **Add overlay...** offers every type; the list is compact.
- [x] **3. Overlays: timeline and distribution.** Subplots stacked with their own scale, legend and value; **Add subplot**, **Up**, **Remove**; **As the movie plays**, **Value**, **Titles**, **Markers**; elapsed vs trajectory axis, backward trajectory still grows to the right; late-appearing overlay already shows the past; subplots that come in at their own times (plate grows, others do not move); distribution counts frames played so far; **Number of bins** 32/37/128 and **Use source bin counts**; marker targets (one subplot vs **All timeline subplots**) and names survive save/load, undo, length change and renaming; look settings (points, colors, plate presets) are the same in a recording at another resolution.
- [x] **4. Overlays: property visualization and the frame.** A property overlay shows highlight, lines and labels in the viewport and in a recording, fades with the overlay and leaves nothing highlighted when it ends. In **Movie preview** the frame follows the resolution (also tall custom sizes); the recording matches it (camera, overlays, scale bar, labels, sharp at 1080p and at **Scale** 25 %, no smearing with **Samples per frame** > 1). Closing the Movie window or recording removes the dimming. Atom picking still hits the right atom.
- [x] **5. Camera keys basics.** **Add Keyframe** and **K** (replaces a key at the same time and keeps its spin and frame); **Key on Selection** (also for a molecule split by the cell); **Copy** / **Paste Keyframe** / Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V; drag a row by its number; **Set Follow Target** with **keys follow target**; **Follow** (after **Go To**) and **Unfollow**; **Look at** an atom tracks it; **Update position**; the follow-aware path in the viewport grows without stalling the UI and matches **Play Preview**.

## B. Camera (to check)

- [ ] **6. Aiming, orbits, loops, upright.** Double-click in the viewport aims at the clicked point with the eye fixed. **Add Orbit** with **Snap to frames** puts the end key where it should be and is refused past the movie end. **Close Loop** and **Seamless loop**: no jump on repeat. **Keep upright**: keys captured tilted play level, spins and loops never flip; **Up** and **From view** pick the axis; **Roll (deg)** tilts and eases, 0 levels; Go To, path cameras and Ctrl+click on the path agree with the preview. Off: **Roll** shows each tilt and **Level** straightens it. Undo/save/load keep it; an old workspace opens with it off.
- [ ] **7. Camera lane.** Orange dots with number and name (yellow at the preview time). Drag a dot (preview follows, list re-sorts on release, snaps to frames, Ctrl+Z undoes); click goes to the key; right-click: type a name (appears next to the number, in the **Name** column, survives save/open), **Go to**, **Remove** (Ctrl+Z restores). Double-click an empty spot adds a key on the path without moving the camera (not where a key is, not while recording). Bands: teal for follow, blue with atom number for **Look at**, purple `+2 x` for spin, green with frame number for pinned frames; hover shows details; a key added between two that follow also follows.
- [ ] **8. Camera path in the viewport.** Blue eye path, yellow look-at path, brighter ahead than behind. Green camera at the preview time (dot, ring with cross and **looks at**, arrow). Ticks at round times with readable labels and chevrons, crowding near an **Ease in/out** key; **Time ticks**, **Sight lines**, **Cameras**, **Spin rings** each switch off and are saved. Handles: numbered eye dot (with name) and ring with cross; white / teal / blue / orange; hover lights the key in the camera lane and vice versa; click goes to the key without selecting or rotating; drag the eye (the ring stays), drag the ring (the eye stays), Ctrl+drag both, Esc cancels, one undo step; works for follow keys too. Ctrl+click on the path shows `add a key at ... s` and adds one without moving the camera. The path is not in recorded frames or screenshots and is hidden while recording and while a key waits for **Look at**. With depth of field on, the pink focus frame is still drawn.

## C. Timeline interaction (to check)

- [ ] **9. Picking, moving, stretching, keyboard.** Click, Ctrl+click and box pick (blue box; camera lane by time only; Ctrl/Shift adds; background click puts all down; the playhead and blue anchors still drag normally). A camera key's ring shows in every lane it is drawn in and around its viewport handle. Group drag in time keeps spacing, stops at the movie ends as a whole, snaps, tooltip `N keys: +x s`, one undo. Group drag in value (trajectory frames, distance, FOV, look parameter, representation property; limits respected; colors and **Visible** move in time only). A moved key landing on another replaces it on release. Bars and blocks: click/Ctrl+click/box pick; dragging by the middle moves everything picked, timed subplot times move with a bar, a block stops at other blocks of its representation, dragging an end changes only that end; Delete removes picked blocks and keys but not overlays. Stretch box (200 %, 50 %, about first item vs about preview time, stops at the movie end, one undo, back to 100 % on release). Inspector line (counts, **first at**, ease for all picked or **(mixed)**, rename one camera key). Keyboard: arrows (Shift second, Ctrl ten frames), up/down values, Delete, Esc, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C/V; buttons **Pick all**, **Put down**, **Delete**, **Copy**, **Paste** do the same; without picked keys arrows and Ctrl+C/V behave as before.
- [ ] **10. Ruler, timing, zoom, lanes.** Ruler: seconds, a tick per frame when zoomed (numbers follow zoom), markers as yellow triangles (hover time, click goes there), click/drag moves the preview time (snapped), red recording time while recording. Every lane shows its name. **Snap to frames** on: keys, parameter and representation keys, blue anchors and the playhead land on frames; off: free. Blue **Start** / **End** anchors (trajectory waits, then holds). **Movie length (s)** doubled: same pacing at half speed, overlays and fades scale, undo restores. Frame pin dragged up/down changes the frame; distance dot keeps looking at the same point; FOV dot changes the angle. Ctrl+wheel zoom, middle button / Shift+wheel / sideways pan, wheel scrolls, **Show whole movie**. **Lane height** (tall lanes, scrolling, long lanes grow only themselves, divider drags resize two lanes), **Fit to window**. Hide each lane (all hidden says so); lanes, height and **Fit to window** survive save/open. Overlay lane bars: drag, drag an end, subplot times move, ticks and markers, snapping, undo. Clicking a key's orange marker in the **Timelines** window goes to the key. Backward trajectory: frame pins with a lower second frame play backward and slow to a stop at the turn; **Trajectory Frames** with start above end plays backward in preview and recording.

## D. Scene view (to check)

- [ ] **11. Scene view, Movie preview, live picture.** First **Switch to Scene view** frames the whole path from your direction, frame and overlays gone; **Fit path** frames it again. With **Animate camera**, scrubbing and playing move the green camera and play the trajectory but not the viewport; clicking a key (lane, table **Go To**, handle) moves only the playhead and green camera. **Switch to Movie preview** returns to the movie camera (at the preview time with **Animate camera**, else the view you had), and back again to your Scene view pose. Tab and hold-Tab (over a third of a second) peek and return; Tab does nothing in a text field or while recording. The path is hidden in Movie preview unless **In Movie preview** is ticked. In Scene view: **K** adds a key on the path without moving the camera (with no keys, from the Movie preview view), **Key on Selection** frames the selection from the movie camera's direction, **Update position** is off. Recording from Scene view uses the movie camera and returns to the Scene view pose. **Preview** picture (lower right of the Movie window, with overlays, movie proportions, absent in Movie preview) follows scrubbing, playing, handle drags and FOV changes; right-click: Small / Medium / Large / Hide (unticks **Preview**); the main viewport is unchanged by it (no flicker or ghosting with TAA, picking and selection still right, depth of field and occlusion same as with **Preview** off); follow keys show the right frame; nothing is rendered for it with the window closed or collapsed; frame rate with **Preview** on is acceptable on a large system.

## E. Representations and looks (to check)

- [ ] **12. Representation keys, overview, swap.** **Visible** keys grow in / shrink away over **Transition (s)** (2 s default; 0 instant; 10 slow; two keys closer than the transition turn around); cartoon/ribbon look right at tiny sizes; a dipole or electronic-structure representation stays full size and vanishes at the end. Scales, **Saturation**, **Tint scale**, **Tint color**, **Base color** (Uniform mapping) key and scrub correctly (the example shrinks the ligand's ball scale 30 s to 38 s). Overview: one row per system (`protein-cartoon`, `protein-cpk`, `protein-vdw` and plain `protein` share a row; renaming regroups), labelled colored blocks, unkeyed enabled representation covers the whole movie, blocks cross-fade, systems separated by gap, divider and shading, **One line per system** on/off. Edit: drag block or edge (neighbours of the same representation constrain), double-click empty space adds, right-click **Remove block** or switch (CPK to VDW interval transfers, overlapping blocks merge, other systems and property keys intact), removing the last block hides it, undo/redo for each, example groups intact. **Swap with the next of its group** works (disabled with one member, undo). Representation lane: pick representation and property, drag in time and value, double-click adds, right-click removes, **Visible** double-click turns around and keys time 0 the first time. Duplicate a keyed representation (copy has no keys), remove one (keys gone). Save/open keeps ids and keys. After recording or **Animate parameters** off the representations are as before; the **Representations** window is locked while recording.
- [ ] **13. Look parameters and depth of field.** Key a parameter at two times and scrub; drag in the lane, double-click adds, right-click removes; **Animate parameters** off lets go of parameters and representations. Depth of field **Focus**: look-at point, distance (key **Focus distance**), follow target; the focus plane shows in magenta.

## F. Output (to check)

- [ ] **14. Recording.** **PNG sequence** (numbered from 0, view and playback restored afterwards); **MP4 H.264** plays; **MP4 H.265** and **WebM VP9** (needs libx265 / libvpx-vp9) play, have the right extension in the log and workspace copy, and the **Quality (CRF)** range changes (0 to 63 for VP9). **Pause** / **Resume** in the window and on the banner: no duplicated or skipped frame. Time left appears after a few frames and is about right. **Scale** 25 %: small even-sized frames, fast, overlays scale. **Samples per frame**: 1 fast and rough, 8+ smooth. **Render only a range** 2 to 4 s: PNG keeps whole-movie numbers (frame 48 is `..._00048.png`), MP4 is 2 s. **Save a workspace copy with the movie** writes `prefix.via` without changing the open workspace's name; opening it and recording gives the same movie. Esc and **Stop Recording** keep the frames so far.
